# FluTrack Copy Slots

The overhaul preserves all existing user-facing wording. The following four visible placeholders are the only new content requirements. They appear only in the state-page season-kit frame, where filling the space with invented health or affiliate copy would violate the content boundary.

| Location | Visible placeholder | Purpose | Tone | Maximum words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| State-page season-kit heading | `[COPY NEEDED: season kit module title, max 8 words]` | Name the three-slot seasonal affiliate module. | Useful, factual, non-alarmist, and clearly separate from surveillance data. | 8 |
| Slot one | `[COPY NEEDED: affiliate product one, max 18 words]` | Identify the first owner-supplied affiliate item. | Plain product identification; no health claim, implication, or recommendation. | 18 |
| Slot two | `[COPY NEEDED: affiliate product two, max 18 words]` | Identify the second owner-supplied affiliate item. | Plain product identification; no health claim, implication, or recommendation. | 18 |
| Slot three | `[COPY NEEDED: affiliate product three, max 18 words]` | Identify the third owner-supplied affiliate item. | Plain product identification; no health claim, implication, or recommendation. | 18 |

The inline label **Affiliate content** is required by the work order to distinguish the module from report data and navigation. The label **Advertisement** is likewise required by the work order for reserved advertising placements. The System, Light, and Dark labels are required by the accessibility floor for the visible theme control.
