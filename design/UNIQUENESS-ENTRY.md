# FluTrack — Portfolio Uniqueness Entry

FluTrack’s identity is the **weekly state bulletin**: an editorial public record rendered on bulletin paper with press-ink rules, an instrument-scale activity number, and a severity language that pairs restrained categorical color with visibly densifying field patterns, glyphs, words, and indices. Newsreader gives its headlines the authority of a newspaper report; Public Sans anchors the interface in federal-data clarity; IBM Plex Mono appears only at the gauge number. The site is purposely still, asymmetric, and rule-led: the answer itself commands the hero, while maps, comparisons, signup, advertisements, and legal cautions occupy measured report territory rather than interchangeable rounded cards. It must not share this editorial-instrument vocabulary, pattern hierarchy, paper/ink palette, or state-bulletin composition with any other property.
